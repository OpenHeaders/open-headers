## Languages

- **Japanese, Korean, Russian and Romanian** — the served tab now
  reads in eight languages beside English: Français, Español,
  Deutsch, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский and Română. The workbench,
  every editor, the settings pages, the docs and diagrams, and the
  server admin pages are translated. Settings › General › Language
  picks one — *Follow system* matches the browser's language — and
  the change applies at once, no reload. Technical vocabulary stays
  in English in every language, and date pickers, tables and
  pagination follow the picked language's pack.
