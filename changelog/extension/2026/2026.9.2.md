## Languages

- **Japanese, Korean, Russian and Romanian** — the extension now
  reads in eight languages beside English: Français, Español,
  Deutsch, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский and Română. The popup, the
  DevTools panel — Network, Console, Storage, the quick editor, the
  docs and the Inspector's Headers, Cookies and Streams tabs — and
  the workbench tab with every editor, settings page, docs page and
  diagram are translated, as are the header, status and cookie
  reference popovers.
- **Picking a language** — Settings › General › Language in the
  workbench tab lists the eight by their own names; *Follow system*
  matches the browser's language. The change applies at once to
  every open surface — popup, panel and tab — no reload. Technical
  vocabulary — header names, HTTP methods, protocol terms — stays in
  English in every language.
- **Component vocabulary follows** — date pickers, tables,
  pagination and the confirm buttons take their words from the
  picked language's pack too, so no dialog reads half in English.
