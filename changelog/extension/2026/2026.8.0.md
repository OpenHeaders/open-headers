## Workbench

- **Bottom panel splits** — the bottom panel can hold two tool
  windows at once, side by side or stacked. Pick the arrangement from
  the merged Bottom Panel Layout menu or the panel toolbar, or drag a
  tab onto a drop zone to split in place — in the workbench and the
  DevTools panel alike.
- **Speed-search** — the sidebar gains an on-demand filter/search
  mode; filtering prunes empty folders and highlights no-match state.
- **Compact sidebar** — denser rows, hover-gated controls, one global
  focus tint, and slimmer dividers across the sidebar trees.
- **Tool windows keep their state** — switching dock tabs no longer
  resets a tool window's scroll and selection; bodies stay mounted
  and pick up where you left them.

## Traffic

- **See what's observed** — tabs being observed for traffic capture
  gather into a blue OpenHeaders AI tab group, and badges mark only
  the tabs that are actually capture-armed. When observation stops,
  the group dissolves and your prior tab grouping is restored.

## Desktop connection

- **Auto-connect** — with your consent, the extension watches for the
  desktop app and connects the moment it becomes available.
- **Launch from where you are** — when the desktop app is installed
  but not running, the connection row offers to launch it directly;
  the affordance appears only when the native launcher is actually
  present.

## Polish

- **Desktop app suggestion** — a sticky card suggests the desktop app
  where it would unlock more, and the suggestions list gets a cleaner
  empty state.
- **Tour dismissal** — the popup tour closes with Esc or its close
  button; the DevTools tour keeps a plain close button, since
  DevTools claims Esc for itself.
- **Smoother scrolling** — wheel scrolling works everywhere in
  request details, including over key-value cells.
