## Commands

- **`oh login [--daemon <url>] [--label <name>]`** — signs a person in
  on the server's own page. It prints the link to open and the code
  the page will show, opens the link when a browser is within reach
  (never over SSH or without a display — paste the link into any
  browser then), waits at the server's interval while you sign in
  there with your credentials or your identity provider and approve
  the device, and saves the credential to `cli.json` exactly as
  `oh connect --token` would. `--label` names the device on the
  consent page and under Paired devices (up to 64 characters). A
  denied, expired or refused sign-in exits with one line on its
  cause. `oh connect --token` stays for tokens an administrator
  minted.
