## Languages

- **Japanese, Korean, Russian and Romanian** — the interface now
  reads in eight languages beside English: Français, Español,
  Deutsch, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский and Română. Every surface
  is translated — the workbench and its sidebar, the editors for
  every request kind (HTTP, GraphQL, WebSocket, gRPC, MQTT, rules
  and specs), the settings pages and their keyboard and DevTools
  definitions, the docs and their diagrams, import and export, the
  live view, the variables pages, script packages, trusted roots,
  the notifications, the conflict and merge editor, and the header,
  status and cookie reference popovers.
- **Picking a language** — Settings › General › Language lists the
  eight by their own names; *Follow system* matches the operating
  system. The change applies at once to every open window, no
  reload. Technical vocabulary — header names, HTTP methods,
  protocol terms — stays in English in every language.
- **Component vocabulary follows** — date pickers, tables,
  pagination and the confirm buttons take their words from the
  picked language's pack too, so no dialog reads half in English.
