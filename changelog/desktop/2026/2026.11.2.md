## Fixes

- **Windows updates were full downloads** — every update fetched the
  whole installer, about 180 MB, where a fraction of it would do. The
  app now finds the copy its installer keeps, so an update downloads
  only what changed. This update is still a full download; the ones
  after it are not, and the next one also removes the folder the old
  downloads sat in (about 190 MB).
- **Windows updates took long to install** — the installer started a
  helper program several times to learn whether the app was still
  running. It now checks that itself. This update installs somewhat
  faster; the ones after it get the whole gain.
