## Windows updates

- **Download only what changed** — an update fetches the parts of the
  installer that differ from the installed version instead of the
  whole installer.
- **Install faster** — the installer checks for a running app by
  itself, with no helper programs to start.
- Both take full effect from the update after this one.

## Platform

- **A smaller app** — the packages carry only what the app runs: less
  to download and less on disk, on Windows, macOS and Linux.
