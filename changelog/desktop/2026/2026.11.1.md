## Traffic

- **A request that ran with DevTools closed says so** — its Response
  and Preview read *Content is available only with DevTools opened, or
  with Debug mode on* instead of loading forever, and the row shows the
  size the response declared. Under the notice sit the browser's
  **Debug mode** switch, **Attach to** and **Include this browser tab**.
- **A long history no longer drops the browser** — watching a tab with
  a large log closed the browser's connection to the app. A response
  too large to cross reads *Content is too large to show here*; with a
  browser extension older than 2026.11.1 the tab shows a notice with
  **Clear log**.
- **Closed to the tray, the window receives nothing** — browser tabs'
  traffic, console and storage stop flowing to a closed window and
  refill when it is opened. Captures and recordings keep running.

## Fixes

- **Traffic after startup** — a window that loaded before the app had
  finished starting could read *No browser connected* until the app
  was restarted.
- **What's New on Windows** — the tab opened on a block of settings
  (`version`, `date`, `channel`…) above the release notes. It now
  starts at the notes, as on macOS and Linux.
- The timing popover no longer widens on a long request name.

## Platform

- **Windows: every program file is signed** — the installers, the
  uninstaller, the installer's own libraries and every executable file
  the app installs carry the publisher's signature, on x64 and on
  ARM64. Earlier builds signed the installers and the app's three main
  executables.
